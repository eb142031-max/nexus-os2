## 1. Purpose

Preserve the three original lineages as independent, versioned source systems while making their approved doctrine and capabilities discoverable for potential use across future META OS products.

This registry is a catalogue and governance contract. It does not assert that source manuals, code, exercises, or historical definitions have been imported or verified. It does not grant an application access merely because an item is listed here.

## 4. Registry record requirements

Every source asset should eventually have:
- stable doctrine_id
- supplied display name and any approved aliases
- founding lineage (Shadow Tiger, Golden Lotus, Void Moon, or unassigned)
- source artifact URI or repository path
- source owner / authorship and rights status
- source version, date, and content digest
- original definition and source excerpt
- intended domain and permitted use
- prohibited uses and safety constraints
- dependencies and compatibility notes
- integration status and approving authority
- change history and supersession links

No source artifact or digest is claimed to exist until it has been ingested and measured.

## 5. Integration lifecycle

REGISTERED → SOURCE_VERIFIED → REVIEWED → COMPATIBILITY_TESTED → APPROVED → ADAPTER_IMPLEMENTED → VALIDATED → ENABLED

Alternative terminal or holding states:
- QUARANTINED — provenance, rights, safety, or integrity issue
- REJECTED — integration proposal declined
- DEPRECATED — replaced, but retained for historical traceability
- REVOKED — integration permission withdrawn

Registration is not approval. Approval is scoped to a specific consuming system, version, purpose, data class, and permission set. Enabling one adapter does not authorize other consumers.

## 7. Safety and domain separation

Martial arts material must be handled as training content with appropriate instructor supervision, safety warnings, and lawful defensive context. Do not turn named methods into instructions for harming people.

The T3 Threat Targeting Table and other security-related material must be classified by intended context before use. Defensive assessment, fictional simulation, and authorized training must remain separate from real-world targeting or unauthorized operations.

Psychological, behavioral, and persona-related applications must not present simulations as clinical diagnoses or as verified facts about real people.

## 8. Open source-ingestion questions

Before marking any doctrine SOURCE_VERIFIED, obtain or locate:
1. the original manuals, notes, diagrams, source code, or other primary artifacts;
2. the user's intended definition and expansion for SEMP9, SPHIE9, VFI9, A9F, C09TE, S3, and T3;
3. lineage ownership for each item;
4. source version/date and any known revisions;
5. which items are doctrine, training curricula, algorithms, software modules, or visual symbols;
6. any rights, confidentiality, or distribution restrictions.

Do not silently normalize spelling, acronym expansion, or terminology.

## 9. Acceptance checks

- All three founding lineages are represented independently.
- Every supplied doctrine name is present exactly as supplied.
- Unknown definitions and lineage mappings are explicitly marked unresolved.
- No consumer is enabled by registry presence alone.
- Original source artifacts remain immutable and recoverable.
- Every integration is version-pinned, scoped, permissioned and auditable.
- Revocation and rollback do not delete or rewrite founding sources.
- Safety and domain-separation checks pass before an adapter can be enabled.
